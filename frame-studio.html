How to use

Draw on the canvas with the pen, shapes or stamps. Use + Blank or Duplicate to add frames, and Play to preview. When done, choose a speed and tap Export video.

FAQ

Why isn't my drawing saved?

Projects are not saved when you leave the page, so export your video before closing.

Which video format do I get?

WebM or MP4, depending on your browser.

What does onion skin do?

It shows a faint copy of the previous frame so you can line up the next one.

Export does nothing?

Your browser may not support video recording. Try Chrome, Edge or Safari.
