How to use

Stopwatch: press Start, then Lap to record split times. Reset clears everything. Timer: set hours, minutes and seconds, or tap a preset, then press Start. A sound and a flashing screen tell you when time is up.

The alarm does not sound. Why?

Browsers block sound until you interact with the page. Tap Start first, and turn up your device volume. If your phone is on silent, the alarm will be quiet.

Will it keep counting if I switch tabs?

Yes. The timer checks the real clock, so it stays accurate. If your phone locks the screen, keep the page open to hear the alarm.

What is the 25-minute preset?

It is a common focus length from the Pomodoro method: work for 25 minutes, then take a short break.

Does it save my times?

No. Times are kept only while the page is open.
