How to use

Tap the keys or type on a keyboard. The result shows as you type. Press = to finish. DEG switches between degrees and radians, and Ans reuses your last answer.

FAQ

Degrees or radians?

The button shows the current mode. Tap it to switch.

How do I use the history?

Tap a past calculation to bring it back.

Why does it show Error?

Check for missing brackets, a divide by zero, or a value outside the function's range, such as the square root of a negative number.
