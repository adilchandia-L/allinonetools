Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information allinonetools (the "site") collects and how it is used. allinonetools is a collection of free web tools run by Adil Chandia. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

1. Summary

  • The tools do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email or payment details.
  • Your files, photos, text and recordings are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Notes in the Notepad, and similar saved content, are kept only in your own browser storage on your device.
  • The site does not use advertising trackers or analytics that we control.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect the contents of files you open, photos you resize or convert, text you paste, recordings you make, or calculations you enter. These stay on your device.

3. Information the hosting service may collect

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. GitHub handles this information under its own privacy statement, which we do not control.

4. Browser storage

The Notepad saves your notes using your browser's local storage, so they remain on that device and browser. You can delete them at any time by using the Delete button in the Notepad or by clearing your browser's site data. We cannot see or recover these notes.

5. Third-party services

  • Image to Text (OCR) loads its reading engine and language data from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr). That network may log standard connection details. The image itself is read in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Doodle Maker and Animation Maker use Claude, an AI service by Anthropic. When these tools run inside claude.ai, your prompts are handled under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. On GitHub Pages they cannot draw, so no prompt is sent.
  • Links to other websites (for example Pexels, Pixabay, YouTube or Wikimedia Commons) take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

6. Cookies

allinonetools does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may still store local data, as described in section 4.

7. Children

The tools are general-purpose and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your choices

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete. For data stored in your browser, use the Notepad's Delete button or clear your browser's data for this site.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
