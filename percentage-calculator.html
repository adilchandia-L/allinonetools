Percentage Calculator

How to use

Pick the mode at the top, type the numbers into the boxes, and read the answer. Each mode shows an example underneath.

FAQ

Which mode do I need?

Use X% of Y for a share of a number, X is what % for a ratio, and % change to compare two values.

Why does it show a dash?

A box is empty, or a value cannot be used, such as a zero divisor.

Can I use negative numbers?

Yes, for % change negative numbers show a decrease.
