Screen Recorder & GIF Maker 🎥

Record your screen, or pick a video file, and turn a short clip into an animated GIF.

1. Record your screen

Your browser will ask which window or screen to share. Choose it, then press Stop when you are done.

2. Make a GIF

How to use

To record, press Start recording, choose the window or screen to share, then press Stop. Save the recording if you want to keep it. To make a GIF, pick the clip you want, choose how many frames per second and the width, set where it starts and how long it lasts, then press Make GIF. Save the file when it's ready.

Why is my GIF big or slow to make?

Longer clips, more frames per second and bigger widths make larger files that take longer. Try 8 frames per second, 320 or 480 px wide and a shorter length.

The screen share button does nothing. Why?

Screen recording needs a desktop browser such as Chrome, Edge or Firefox, and some phones and tablets do not support it. Recording may also be blocked inside some preview windows, so try opening the page in a new tab.

Why is the recording a WebM file?

WebM is the format most browsers record in. It plays in most video players. For sharing on a phone or in a chat, the GIF is usually easier.

Does it save my recordings?

No. Recordings and GIFs stay on your device only until you close the page, so save anything you need first.
