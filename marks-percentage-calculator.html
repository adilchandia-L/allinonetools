How to use

Pick your class. Type the name of each subject, the marks you got, and the maximum marks for that subject. Add or remove subjects as needed. Your percentage, grade and pass or fail status update as you type.

FAQ

How is the percentage worked out?

Total marks you got, divided by total maximum marks, times 100. Subjects with different maximum marks are handled correctly.

What is the passing mark?

This page uses 33% as the pass mark, which is common in India. Your school may set its own.

Why is my grade different from my school's?

Grade scales vary between boards and schools. Choose the scale that matches your report card, or use the percentage to check against your school's chart.

Does it save my marks?

No. Marks are only kept while the page is open, so write down your result if you need it later.
