Image to Text (OCR) 🖼️➜📝

Upload a photo or scan of text, and get the words back as editable text.

How to use

Tap the box and choose a picture of your text. Pick the language the text is written in, then tap Read text. Wait for the progress bar to finish, then copy or save the words. Check the result, because very blurry or handwritten text may come out wrong.

Why is the first reading slow?

The first time you use a language, the page downloads its reading data, which needs an internet connection. After that, it runs faster.

Which photos work best?

Flat, sharp, evenly lit pictures with dark text on a light background. Avoid shadows, tilted angles and very small writing.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

The text is read inside your browser, and the image is not stored by this page after you close it.

Can it read handwriting?

It works best with printed text. Neat handwriting may work, but messy handwriting often gives errors.
