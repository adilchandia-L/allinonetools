Weight Calculator

BMI is a rough screening number. It ignores muscle, age and body type, so ask a health professional about your own situation.

How to use

On Convert weight, type an amount and pick its unit to see it in other units. On BMI and healthy range, choose metric or imperial, enter height and weight, and read your BMI and typical weight range.

FAQ

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It is a rough screening number that ignores muscle and body type.

Can I switch units?

Yes. Change the units in the BMI tab and your numbers convert.

Is this medical advice?

No. Speak to a health professional about your health.
