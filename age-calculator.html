Age Calculator

How to use

Enter a date of birth. The 'Age on this date' box starts as today, so change it to find age on another day. Tap Calculate age.

FAQ

Can I find someone else's age?

Yes. Change the date of birth to theirs.

Why does my birthday count differ?

People born on 29 February are counted on 1 March in years without a leap day.

Is the date of birth a required field?

Yes. Enter it to see a result.
