YouTube Thumbnail Downloader 🖼️

Paste a YouTube link. Get the thumbnail in every size, then open it and save.

Tap Open to view the image full size, then long-press it (on phone) or right-click it (on computer) and choose Save. Some videos do not have the largest size. If so, use a smaller one.

How to use

Copy a link from YouTube, including normal videos, Shorts and youtu.be links, and paste it into the box. Tap Find thumbnails. Choose a size and tap Open. Then save the image from the new tab.

Which size should I pick?

Maximum size (1280 × 720) is best for quality. If it is missing for that video, pick Large (640 × 480) or High (480 × 360).

Why does it say the link is not valid?

Check that the link is from YouTube and includes the video. You can also paste just the 11-character video code.

Can I use these images anywhere?

The thumbnails belong to the video's creator. Use them for personal reference, or ask the creator's permission before publishing them.

Does it save my links?

No. Nothing is stored after you close the page.
