How to use

Paste your text, choose how many tags you want, and tap Extract tags. The most useful words and any hashtags you wrote appear as chips. Tap a chip to leave it out. Then copy the list into your platform.

Where do the tags come from?

Words that appear often in your text and are not common filler words like "the" or "and". Hashtags you already wrote are always included.

Why are some words missing?

Very short words and common words are skipped. You can add any word yourself by typing it into the copy box after you copy.

Will these tags help my views?

They describe your content, which helps people find it. No tool can guarantee results, so choose tags that really match the video.

Does it save my text?

No. Nothing is stored after you close the page.
