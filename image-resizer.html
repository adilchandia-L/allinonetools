How to use

Choose a picture. Type a new width or height, or tap a preset such as Instagram or a passport photo. With Keep proportions on, the other size changes to match, so the picture is not stretched. Pick a format and quality, tap Resize to see the new size and file size, then Download.

Which format should I choose?

JPG gives the smallest files for photos. PNG keeps sharp edges and text, but files are bigger. WebP is small and sharp, but some older apps won't open it.

Why did my picture get smaller in file size but look a bit softer?

Lower quality saves space by losing some detail. Raise the quality slider to keep more detail.

Will enlarging make it sharper?

No. Making a picture bigger than the original can only stretch the detail that is already there, so it may look soft.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Resizing happens in your browser, and nothing is stored after you close the page.
