How to use

Type the loan amount, the yearly interest rate, and how long you have to repay. Pick Years or Months for the time. Your monthly payment (EMI) and total cost appear straight away, with a yearly breakdown below.

What is EMI?

EMI means equated monthly installment, the fixed amount you pay each month until the loan is repaid. Part of each payment is interest and part repays the loan.

Why does interest matter so much?

Interest is charged on what you still owe. Over a long loan, the total interest can be far more than the amount you borrowed. Shorter loans usually cost less overall.

Is this the exact amount my bank will charge?

No. This is an estimate. Banks may add fees, insurance or different rates, so check the loan offer before you sign.

Does it save my numbers?

No. Values are only kept while the page is open.
