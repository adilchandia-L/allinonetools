Background Remover ✂️

Removes a plain or solid-colour background from a picture, and saves it as a transparent PNG. Everything runs on your device.

Background colour
Tolerance 40

How to use

Choose a picture. The page guesses the background colour from the corners. If that's wrong, tap Pick colour from picture, then tap the background on the image. Raise the tolerance to remove more shades, or lower it to protect edges. Tap the picture to remove that area, and use Undo if you make a mistake. Then download the PNG.

Why does it work best on plain backgrounds?

This tool removes areas of one colour that connect to the edges. It doesn't understand people or objects, so busy or patterned backgrounds won't come out clean.

Why are some edges rough?

Hair, fur and soft shadows blend into the background. Lower the tolerance a little, or remove the background in parts.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. The picture is processed in your browser and is not stored after you close the page.
