How to use

Paste your caption and tap Analyse caption. You'll see the hashtags, mentions, links and emojis it contains, how many characters it uses, and where the feed cuts it off. Edit your text if needed, then tap Analyse again.

What is the character limit?

Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters. The bar turns orange when you're close to that limit.

Why does the preview cut off?

Instagram usually shows only the first line or about 125 characters before "more". Put your key message at the start.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram currently allows up to 30 hashtags per post. Use the ones that really match your post.

Does it save my text?

No. Nothing is stored after you close the page. This tool does not connect to Instagram.
