Notepad 📝

How to use

Tap New note to start a page, then type a title and your text. Notes save automatically in this browser as you type. Pick any note on the left to open it, use the search box to find one, and use Download or Copy to take the text out.

Are my notes saved?

Yes, they are kept in this browser on this device, so they will still be there when you come back. Clearing your browser data or using a different device will not show them.

How do I delete a note?

Open the note and tap Delete. It asks you to confirm first, and it cannot be undone.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The list moves above the note on small screens. Download saves a plain text file you can open in any app.
