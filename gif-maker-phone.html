GIF Maker 📱

Turn a short video from your phone into an animated GIF in a few taps.

1. Get a video

  1. Use your phone's own screen recorder, or record a clip with the camera below.
  2. Tap Choose video and pick it from your gallery.

2. Choose the part you want

3. Your GIF

How to use

Pick a video from your gallery, or record one. Move the Start and Length sliders to choose the part you want, pick a speed and size, then tap Make GIF. When it's ready, tap Share or save to send it to a chat, or Download to keep it.

How do I record my phone screen?

On most phones, swipe down to open quick settings and tap Screen record. On Samsung, use the Screen recorder in quick settings. Record your steps, stop, then choose the video here.

The GIF is too big to send. What do I do?

Choose Small size, Small file speed, and a shorter length. Smaller files send more easily.

Why is making the GIF slow?

It takes longer on older phones and for longer clips. Keep clips short, around 3 to 5 seconds, for the quickest result.

Does it upload my video anywhere?

No. The work happens on your phone, and nothing is saved after you close the page.
