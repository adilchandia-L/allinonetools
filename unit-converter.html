How to use

Choose a category, such as length or temperature. Pick the unit you have in From, and the unit you want in To. Type your number and the answer updates straight away. The list below shows the value in every unit in that category. Use ⇄ to swap the two units.

Why are some results long decimals?

Some conversions don't divide evenly, so the result is rounded to a sensible number of decimal places.

Are temperatures handled differently?

Yes. Temperature uses a formula rather than a simple multiplication, so 0 °C becomes 32 °F, not 0.

Are US and UK units different?

Yes, for some units. Gallons, pints and fluid ounces differ between US and imperial measures. This tool uses US gallons and pints.

Does it save anything?

No. Nothing is stored after you close the page.
