How to use

Type or paste into the box. Words, characters, sentences and paragraphs update automatically. To stay under a length, pick a limit from the menu, or choose Custom and type your own number. The bar fills up as you approach the limit and turns red if you go over.

Do spaces count as characters?

Yes, in the main Characters count they do. The "no spaces" count leaves them out.

How is reading time worked out?

It assumes an average adult reads about 200 words per minute. Speaking is slower, so a talk takes longer than the reading time.

Are emojis counted?

Emojis count as characters, so a single emoji may count as more than one.

Does it save my text?

No. Text stays only while the page is open, so copy anything you need before closing.
