Before you use anything

1. Check the license on the file's page, not just the website's general terms.
2. Give credit when the license asks for it, and keep the link to the license.
3. Do not use a file for purposes the license forbids, such as commercial use if it says non-commercial.
4. If you cannot find the license, do not use the file.
